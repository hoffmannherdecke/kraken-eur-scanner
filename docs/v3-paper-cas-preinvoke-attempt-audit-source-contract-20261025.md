## Existing owner integration (not a separate audit service)

A future separately authorized **existing exclusive Cloud CAS executor** must satisfy every item **before gaining permission to invoke the V3 CAS RPC**:

Register one reviewed `V3CAS-AUDIT-<32 lowercase hex>` epoch binding the exact existing predecessor `PAPER-V2R4-20261009T110135Z`, expected future RPC and the originalhost source SHA/physical fence. The evidence baseline must be established **before** any route/credential can invoke the RPC. A historical gap cannot be backfilled by a new epoch.
2. **Deny bypass:** the future CAS RPC must not be executable via the shared `service_role` or generic Data API role; an approved isolated invocation identity and comprehensive effective EXECUTE grants need independent live readback, including direct PostgREST/SQL, Edge, owners, elevated/admin escape routes and replica/branch confusion. No public `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC may be left callable to PUBLIC/anon/authenticated. Explicit approved exception paths must fail closed and be audited rather than silently assumed absent.
3. Pre-call append an **immutable, unique, monotonically ordered intent** via the existing authorized Cloud owner into a separately committed, access-controlled append-only audit store. The ACK must be issued **after durable commit**, **before** the CAS RPC can be invoked. This write must not share the CAS transaction: a CAS rollback, timeout, crash or unknown result must not erase the intent. A missing ACK must prevent the RPC entirely, and an ACK whose delivery was uncertain must keep both old and new Writers fenced until independent reconciliation.
4. Intents cannot be removed or edited by the RPC execution identity, Edge handler or ordinary caller. Enforce uniqueness, continuous sequence/epoch coverage, clock/source consistency and anti-replay at the trusted audit store; authenticated retrieval must return **all** intent records from epoch start. A missing record, gap, unknown outcome, TTL expiry, credential drift, reboot or audit outage triggers HOLD, not a zero-intent certificate.
5. **At attempt time, if ANY intent exists (including one yielding NOT_COMMITTED, exception or timeout), old native auto-rearm is forbidden.** Independent Cloud readback is then necessary to reconcile single-active-series state, but does not retroactively undo a CAS attempt or authorize PRE-COMMIT restore.
6. A reviewer may inspect a zero-intent epoch only after independent **exclusive-owner/coverage and issuer** evidence plus current Cloud and originalhost fresh readbacks. The resulting purely parsed payload is not itself proof of its own provenance and never gives Win32/Task privileges.
